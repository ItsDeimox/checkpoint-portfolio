* Red body/hood paint, dark wheels and wing, glass and light materials.
* Front-wheel steering and emissive inserts over the modeled headlamp lenses.
* Recentering, scale and orientation for the DXT showroom.
* These showroom changes are baked offline into nissan-s15-showroom.glb with
  tools/bake-showroom-car.mjs, preserving all prepared triangles and original
  texture bytes. The unchanged source GLB remains available for reproducibility.
