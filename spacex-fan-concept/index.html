An independent interactive concept by Deimox. A quiet, cinematic space to explore the machines that change our relationship with space.

This is a fan-made design study. It is not affiliated with, commissioned by, or endorsed by SpaceX. SpaceX and vehicle names belong to their respective owners.

THE MODELS

Falcon 9: licensed model by Stanley Creative. Falcon Heavy: adapted from those detailed Falcon cores, with original side nosecones. Starship Block 3: licensed model by Clarence365. Falcon 1 and Crew Dragon retain original procedural reconstructions. These are visualization models, not official engineering CAD. Materials and display poses are artistic adaptations. Carousel previews are independently framed, not a scale comparison.

CONTROLS

Drag to rotate. Select a component to inspect. Click empty space or press Escape to return. Use arrows or swipe to change rockets. The component controls also work with keyboard and touch.
