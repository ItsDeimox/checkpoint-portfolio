THE MODELS

Only authored, licensed spacecraft geometry is displayed. Falcon 9: Stanley Creative. Falcon Heavy uses Antonio_Adams’ exterior and Giroo’s center-core barrel for tank inspection. Starship Block 3: Clarence365. Dragon 2: KUBAHA, with its authored docking hardware, nose cover and heat shield; no modeled cabin is supplied. Internal Structure reveals the Falcon fairing wall and payload adapter, engine and interstage hardware, and the Starship tank domes, transfer tubes, cargo rack and booster engines. The Falcon Heavy uses Stanley Creative’s model for the shared standard fairing and second-stage hardware. Separate Merlin 1D and vacuum-engine inspection models are by MartianDays (CC BY 4.0). These expose their supplied exterior hardware, not hidden turbine internals. These are partial artist-model assemblies, not complete manufacturer CAD. No procedural spacecraft, invented internals or fabricated fallback models are used. Falcon 1 is unavailable until a suitable reusable asset is verified. These are artistic reference models, not official engineering CAD. Carousel previews are independently framed, not a scale comparison.

CONTROLS

Drag horizontally to browse the fleet. Select a component, then drag in any direction to rotate relative to the screen. Scroll or pinch to zoom, double-click to reset. Shift-drag or right-drag rotates the full rocket. Click empty space or press Escape to return. Arrow keys and component controls also work with keyboard and touch.
