# Interactive 3D product visualization | capability brief

Provider: Deimox. Status: internal draft, not sent to a recipient.

## Service

Implementation and integration of lightweight browser-based 3D experiences for communication, product exploration and presentations. The customer supplies or approves models, identity and technical copy. Work covers presentation, component inspection, controls, responsive layout, performance budgets and deployment.

Demonstrated capabilities: cinematic materials and bounded HDR optics, source-authored component inspection, smooth transitions, touch/mouse/keyboard controls, adaptive graphics and idle-render pause. An allowlisted message interface supports host integration; an HTML catalogue offers an alternative to WebGL. Internal views expose only supplied geometry.

## Proposed engagement

1. Discovery: audience, use case, asset/rights inventory, supported devices and measurable acceptance criteria. Do not request restricted engineering data before authorization and handling requirements are agreed.
2. Paid pilot: one approved product and bounded modules on a staging page; versioned deliverables, test evidence and review.
3. Production and optional maintenance: host integration, device validation, handover and update/support process.

Negotiate a scoped implementation fee with milestones. Hosting, asset purchases and optional maintenance are separate. No price, deadline, service-level guarantee or exclusivity is promised without discovery and written agreement.

Code may be licensed or assigned only after chain-of-title review. Third-party software, fonts, model licenses and marks remain distinct; no exclusive rights to CC BY assets or customer content are offered.

This is not a certified training system, engineering simulator or digital twin. No manufacturer endorsement, flight-accurate internal reconstruction, proprietary CAD access, guaranteed frame rate or conversion improvement is claimed. The SpaceX demo is an independent fan concept, not permission to commercially use the brand. The generic session adapter is implemented; full renderer generalization remains a separate work item.
