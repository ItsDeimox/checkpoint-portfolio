# Model acquisition review

Date: 2026-11-03. These decisions distinguish downloaded geometry from search thumbnails. No purchases, account access bypasses or outreach were made.

## Accepted for separate inspection

MartianDays Merlin 1D (10,380 source triangles) and Merlin Vacuum (13,422) provide preauthored upper assemblies/nozzles. Creator pages and embedded author/license/source metadata were compared. They are included as independent exterior references, not substituted into the existing multi-engine rocket assemblies and not represented as complete engine internals.

## Not integrated as a new faithful rocket

- Clarence365 Falcon 9 V4 and Falcon Heavy V4: creator descriptions expressly qualify the interiors as inaccurate. Extra internal surfaces are not evidence of exact flight configuration.
- Clarence365 S24/B7 V4: downloaded the GLB and examined separate booster, ship and engine meshes. Its modeled landing-leg/payload interpretations were not validated against the flown vehicle. It was not transplanted into Block 3 or added as an exact flight reference.
- dunealex LT SpaceX Falcon Heavy: downloaded model exposes Kerbal-game part identifiers and game assemblies. Not accepted as real-vehicle engineering geometry.
- Giroo Falcon Heavy: downloaded and viewed the separate second-stage tank/engine. Simplified geometry and stylized internal texturing were not a sufficient fidelity upgrade.
- artemycz Falcon 9: downloaded and examined the engine group. Higher total polygon count did not provide the requested complete engine/tank internals.
- Alternate AllThingsSpace/KUBAHA rocket USDZs: inspected source mesh/material structures. Did not establish complete interiors or a better approved exterior.
- Nick Henning Starhopper mesh distributed by pasalvetti: downloaded original OBJ and rendered the underside. The model body lacked the engine needed for the requested complete view, so no empty-bottom new vehicle was added.
- KUBAHA detailed Merlin, VoitAa Raptor 3 and Stanley Creative Falcon 1: listings found, but no verified reusable downloadable file was acquired through the available authorized path. This does not mean those models do not exist.
- Several printable-model sources did not yield accessible source files. No STL was invented from their thumbnails. KSP configs with a permissive license were not treated as permission to redistribute separately licensed game assets.
